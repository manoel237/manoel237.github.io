Software · 2025 – 2026

Automated pre-filtering of high-speed video

A Python tool that finds the relevant events in high-speed camera recordings and discards false triggers.

  • Divides each frame into slices and tracks their luminosity over thousands of frames
  • Builds space–time luminosity maps to locate when and where an event happens
  • Interactive viewer to review and edit each detected event
  • Less manual review time and less storage for irrelevant recordings
